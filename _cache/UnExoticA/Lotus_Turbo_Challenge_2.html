Game/Leitch_Barry/Lotus_2.lha

Download archive Game/Leitch_Barry/Lotus_2.lha
The composer writes, "The infamous one with the subliminal speech. The Speech says 'you will not copy this game', we originally had a bit of speech that said 'You must kill mommy and daddy' but we didn't have the balls to leave that in the game..."

Included in the Bonus directory are some XM remixes of the ending music by Barry who says, "The completion music was the Top Gear Title Music in the SNES and I remixed it for Top Gear Rally on the N64, but never used it"
