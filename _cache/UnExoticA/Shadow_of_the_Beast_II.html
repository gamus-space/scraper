Note that the actual Game Over music is modified Soundtracker which will not play as intended. It's included in this format, along with a Custom version, and as a reconstructed version in NoiseTracker.

The composer proudly says, "[This was] my first ever commercial Amiga Project. And what a project to cut your teeth on... the day I got offered this job I just couldn't believe my luck!"

Also included are the original MODs direct from Tim.
